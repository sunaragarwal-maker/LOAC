Our story

Made by hand, given with heart.

Life on a Canvas is a small handmade gift studio creating personalised pieces for kids and families — each one made to be treasured.

A creative passion, turned into something special

Every dream starts with a single step. Mine started with a passion for creating. What began as a hobby at my craft table has grown into a business driven by creativity, purpose, and the joy of making people smile. Every handcrafted piece tells a story and is made with love, attention to detail, and a personal touch that no machine can replace.

Today, I don't just create products—I create meaningful gifts, lasting memories, and fun, creative experiences for children and families through my workshops and handmade collections. Here's to creating, inspiring, and growing—one handmade piece at a time. ✨💛

A note on our process

Every order starts with a conversation. We want to understand who the gift is for, what they love, and what would make them smile when they open it. Then we get to work — painting, crafting, personalising — until it's ready to go.

Because everything is made to order, please allow 3–5 days for production before shipping. Delivery then takes another 5–7 business days, so plan ahead for special occasions!
