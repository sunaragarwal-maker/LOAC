FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about ordering from us.

How do I place an order? +

Click any "Add to cart" button on our products and use the cart to place a WhatsApp order. Or tap "Chat on WhatsApp" any time to start a conversation directly — we're quick to reply!

Can I get a product personalised with a name? +

Absolutely — personalisation is our specialty! Most products can be customised with your child's name, initials, a special message, or a favourite design. Just mention your requirements when you WhatsApp us.

Do you ship across India? +

Yes! We ship pan-India. Delivery typically takes 5–7 business days after production (which takes 3–5 days). Shipping is charged separately on top of the product price — the exact cost depends on your location and is confirmed on WhatsApp before payment.

What are the payment options? +

We accept UPI only. Payment details are shared on WhatsApp after you confirm your order. We do not accept cash on delivery or bank transfer currently.

Can I order in bulk for events or return gifts? +

Yes! We love doing bulk orders for birthdays, school events, and weddings. Reach out on WhatsApp with the quantity and occasion and we'll help plan it for you.

What is your return policy? +

Since all products are handmade and personalised, returns are not accepted except in case of damage during delivery. Take a video during unboxing. If any damage occurs in transit, the product will be replaced or refunded. See our Refund Policy for full details.
