Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about ordering.

How do I place an order? +

Click any "Order on WhatsApp" button on the site. It will open a WhatsApp chat with the message pre-filled. Just send it and we'll get back to you with availability, customisation options, and payment details.

Do you ship across India? +

Yes! We ship pan-India. Delivery typically takes 5–7 business days depending on your location. Shipping charges (if any) will be confirmed on WhatsApp before payment.

Can I get a product personalised with a name? +

Absolutely — personalisation is our specialty! Most products can be customised with your child's name, a special message, or a favourite character. Just mention your requirements when you WhatsApp us.

What are the payment options? +

We accept UPI and bank transfer. Payment details are shared on WhatsApp after you confirm your order. We do not accept cash on delivery.

Can I order in bulk for events or return gifts? +

Yes! We love doing bulk orders for birthdays, school events, and weddings. Reach out on WhatsApp with the quantity and occasion and we'll help plan it for you.

What if I need to return or exchange my order? +

Since all products are handmade and personalised, returns are not accepted except in case of damage during delivery. Please share photos within 48 hours of receiving the order and we'll sort it out.
