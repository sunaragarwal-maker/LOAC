Privacy Policy

A plain-language explanation of what information we collect and how it's used. Last updated July 2026.

What we collect

When you place an order — whether through our website's order form or directly on WhatsApp — we collect the information you share with us, such as your name, phone number, delivery address, and any personalisation details (like a name or message to feature on your product).

How we use it

We use this information only to process your order, communicate with you about it, personalise your product correctly, and arrange delivery. We do not use your information for anything beyond fulfilling and supporting your order.

What we don't do

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Your details are only shared with our courier partners, and only to the extent needed to deliver your order to you.

Payment information

Payments are made directly via UPI, arranged with you on WhatsApp. We do not collect or store your card, bank, or UPI credentials on our website — the website's cart is only used to prepare your WhatsApp order message.

Data retention

We keep your order details only as long as needed to fulfil your order and handle any follow-up questions, warranty, or refund requests. You can ask us to delete your information at any time by contacting us.

Cookies & analytics

Our website uses basic analytics (Google Analytics) to understand overall site traffic, such as how many people visit and which pages are popular. This does not identify you personally.

Questions or requests

For any privacy questions, or to request that we delete your information, contact us on WhatsApp.
