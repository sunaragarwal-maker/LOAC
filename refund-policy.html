Refund Policy

Since every item is handmade to order, our refund policy works a little differently to off-the-shelf shopping. Last updated July 2026.

Made-to-order, so no general returns or exchanges

Because each product is handcrafted and personalised specifically for you (often with a name, colour, or design of your choice), we are unable to accept returns, exchanges, or refunds once production has started — except in the case of damage or a manufacturing defect, described below.

Cancellations

You can cancel or change your order for a full refund only before production begins. Once we've started crafting your piece, it can no longer be cancelled since it's already personalised for you. Message us on WhatsApp as soon as possible if you need to make a change.

Damaged or defective items

We package every order carefully, but if your item arrives damaged or with a manufacturing defect:

  • Please record an unedited unboxing video showing the sealed package being opened — this is required as proof of damage.
  • Contact us on WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery with the video and photos of the issue.
  • Once verified, we'll offer a free replacement or a full refund, whichever you prefer.

Claims made without an unboxing video, or after 48 hours of delivery, unfortunately cannot be honoured.

Refund method & timeline

Approved refunds are sent back via UPI to the account payment was originally made from, within 5–7 business days of approval.

Questions?

Reach out to us anytime on WhatsApp and we'll be happy to help.
