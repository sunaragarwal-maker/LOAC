Shipping Policy

Everything to know about production time, delivery, and shipping charges. Last updated July 2026.

Made-to-order production time

Every piece is handmade after you order — nothing sits pre-made on a shelf. Please allow 3–5 business days for production before your order ships. During festive season or for large/bulk orders, production may take a little longer; we'll let you know on WhatsApp if that's the case.

Delivery time

We ship pan-India via trusted courier partners. Once your order is dispatched, delivery typically takes another 5–7 business days, depending on your location. A tracking link is shared on WhatsApp as soon as your order ships.

Shipping charges

Shipping is not included in the product price shown on our site — it's an additional cost on top. The exact amount depends on your delivery location and the size/weight of your order, and is confirmed on WhatsApp before you make payment. There are no hidden charges — you'll always know the total before you pay.

Order tracking

Once your order ships, we'll send you the courier name and tracking number on WhatsApp so you can follow its progress right up to your doorstep.

Delays

While we do our best to meet the timelines above, factors like courier delays, remote pin codes, weather, or festive-season order volumes can occasionally push delivery back. If you're ordering for a specific date (like a birthday or festival), please message us in advance so we can plan accordingly.

Questions?

Reach out to us anytime on WhatsApp and we'll be happy to help.
